# Privacy Policy: Should I Watch for Netflix

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Should I Watch for Netflix is a browser extension that shows ratings and a verdict when you hover over a title on Netflix.

## What the extension stores

Your settings, any API keys you enter, a cache of lookup results, your thumbs-up/down ratings (the "taste profile") and saved AI opinions are stored in `chrome.storage.local` on your device. The developer cannot see this data and it is not sent to the developer.

## What the extension sends

- **When you hover a title:** the title name and year are sent to the public rating services AniList, Cinemeta, and, if you entered keys, MyAnimeList, TMDB and OMDb. If you entered a key for a service, it is sent to that service to authenticate your request.
- **When you click the AI button:** the title details and your taste profile are sent to the AI provider you configured (DeepSeek by default, or a custom endpoint you set), together with your API key for that provider.

These services have their own privacy policies. The extension sends nothing to them until you hover a title (or click the AI button).

## What the extension does not do

It has no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising. It does not collect your Netflix account details, viewing history or personal information. It does not sell or share your data, and it does not load remote code.

## Your control

You can turn the extension off with the switch in its popup. You can delete your ratings and saved AI opinions with "Clear the whole taste profile" and clear cached results with "Clear cache" on the options page. Uninstalling the extension removes all stored data.
